Student Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about finding and connecting with student flatmates.

Is Roommate Finder free for students?

Yes, 100% free! College students can create profiles, search listings, and contact potential roommates without any brokerage or subscription fees.

Does my newly created profile stay saved?

Yes! Profiles are saved directly inside your browser's persistent LocalStorage and synced with the SQLite database when running with the backend server.

How do I contact another student?

Click the "Contact" button on any profile card to view their verified student phone number, email address, or open a pre-filled direct WhatsApp chat.

Which Shirpur localities are covered?

We specifically cover all primary student residential clusters around R. C. Patel campus in Shirpur including Near College, Nimzari Naka, Karvand Road, Kazi Nagar, Sandipani Colony, and Sandipani Colony 2.
